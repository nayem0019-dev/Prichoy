Return Policy

🎯 Simple rule: If something's wrong with your order, we'll make it right. We process return requests within 48 hours of receiving the item back.

What can be returned?

  • Items with manufacturing defects or damage
  • Wrong item sent (different from what was ordered)
  • Items that don't match the product description

Return conditions

  • Request within 7 days of delivery
  • Items must be unworn, unwashed, with tags attached
  • Original packaging preferred but not required
  • Contact us via Facebook Messenger or WhatsApp before sending

What happens after return?

Once we receive and inspect the item, we will offer an exchange for available stock or a refund via bKash/Nagad. Processing time is 2-3 business days after receipt.

⚠️ Non-returnable items

Sale / discounted items, items marked as final sale, and items without original packaging in used condition are not eligible for return.

Exchange Policy

👕 Wrong size? Exchange it! We make size exchanges easy because getting the right fit matters.

Exchange process

  • Contact us within 7 days of delivery via Facebook Messenger or WhatsApp
  • Specify the size/colour you need
  • We confirm availability and arrange courier pickup
  • Replacement ships within 24 hours of receiving the original

Exchange courier charges

First exchange — on us!

We cover the courier cost for your first size exchange on any order. Subsequent exchanges on the same order: ৳80 within Dhaka, ৳120 outside Dhaka.

Exchange availability

Exchanges are subject to stock availability. If the requested size/colour is not available, we will offer a full refund or store credit.

Privacy Policy

What data we collect

  • Name, phone number, and delivery address (required for orders)
  • Email address (optional — for order confirmations)
  • Order history and preferences
  • IP address and device info for fraud prevention

How we use your data

  • Processing and delivering your orders
  • Sending order status updates via WhatsApp/SMS
  • Improving our products and services
  • Fraud detection and prevention

Data sharing

We share your delivery address with our courier partners only. We never sell your personal data to third parties. Your phone number may be used by the courier to coordinate delivery.

Your rights

You may request deletion of your account and personal data by contacting us. Orders placed will be retained for 3 years for legal and tax compliance.

Cookies

We use minimal localStorage (not tracking cookies) to save your cart and preferences locally. No third-party tracking cookies are used.

Security

🔒 Your security is our responsibility. We never ask for your bank PIN, OTP, or card number — if anyone claiming to be Prichoy asks for these, it's a scam.

How we protect you

  • All passwords hashed with bcrypt (never stored in plain text)
  • HTTPS encryption for all data in transit
  • Rate limiting on order placement to prevent abuse
  • Order prices are always verified server-side — tampered requests are corrected
  • Blacklist system to protect against fraudulent orders

Safe shopping tips

  • Only shop at prichoy.com or our verified Facebook page
  • We never contact you via SMS asking for personal info
  • If you receive a suspicious call claiming to be Prichoy, call us back on our official number
  • Check that the URL starts with https:// before entering any information

Report a security issue

Found a vulnerability? Please contact us privately at our support channels below. We appreciate responsible disclosure.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I track my order?

Go to our Track Order page and enter your order number and phone number. You'll see the full timeline from placement to delivery. You can also check via WhatsApp message on 01762647661.

Can I change my order after placing it?

Yes, if the order hasn't been dispatched yet. Contact us immediately via Facebook Messenger or WhatsApp. Once dispatched, the order cannot be modified.

Do you deliver outside Bangladesh?

Currently we deliver within Bangladesh only. International shipping is on our roadmap.

How accurate are the product photos?

We photograph all products with natural lighting to represent colours accurately. Slight variations may occur due to screen calibration. If the delivered item significantly differs from the photos, we'll exchange it free of charge.

What if I'm not home for delivery?

The courier will attempt delivery and contact you. You can reschedule by calling the courier or contacting us. Undelivered packages are returned to us after 3 attempts.

Can I order in bulk or wholesale?

Yes! Contact us via Facebook Messenger or WhatsApp for wholesale pricing and minimum order quantities.

How do I know my review will be genuine?

Only customers with a confirmed delivered order can submit reviews, and all reviews go through moderation before appearing. We never incentivise or edit positive reviews.

Is my payment information safe?

Since most orders are Cash on Delivery, you never enter payment details on our site. For bKash payments, you initiate the transfer directly in the bKash app — we never handle your bKash PIN.
